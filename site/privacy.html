CareLoop is a hackathon demo that uses synthetic data. Don't enter real health information.

Last updated: October 16, 2026.

What CareLoop keeps

  • Care plan items you approve, each with the short line of the document it came from.
  • Your check-ins (for example a pain score), completed tasks, alerts and acknowledgements.
  • The people you invite, what you allowed each of them to do, and when that access ends.
  • Questions you save for your clinician, and visit handoffs you create.
  • Your display name and time zone, if you set them.
  • A log of what family members viewed or did (never the content), so you can ask who accessed your data.

Uploaded documents

When you share a prescription or plan, CareLoop reads it on its own server (text extraction, and OCR for scans or photos) with a local AI model. The extracted text is kept for at most 24 hours while you review it. Only the lines behind items you approve are kept after that. The uploaded file itself is not stored.

Why

To let you and the people you choose coordinate your care, and to give your clinician an accurate summary. CareLoop does not give medical advice, diagnose, or monitor for emergencies.

Who can see it

Only you, and the people you invite, limited to the permissions you confirm. Shared speakers never read out health values or plan details. CareLoop does not sell or share your data with anyone else.

How long

Records are kept for up to 365 days unless you delete them sooner. Deleted records are removed from search immediately.

Your choices

You can export your data, delete records, revoke anyone's access at any time, and ask who has accessed your data.

Contact

This demo is maintained by its repository owner. Open an issue in the project repository to ask a question.
